Kashkar Analitika

Kashkar Analitika is an internal tool of Kashkar Magazin, a clothing and goods store in Osh, Kyrgyzstan. It is used only by the store owner to manage the store’s own Instagram Business account @kashkar_magazin:

  • view account and post insights (reach, views, followers);
  • publish the store’s own Reels and Stories;
  • read comments and direct messages sent to the store and reply to customers quickly (prices, availability, address, WhatsApp contact).

The app is not offered to other businesses or the public.
