Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

This policy describes how Kashkar Analitika (“the app”), operated by Kashkar Magazin, Osh, Kyrgyzstan, handles information obtained through the Instagram API.

What we access

  • Insights, posts and profile data of our own Instagram Business account @kashkar_magazin.
  • Comments on our posts and direct messages sent to our account: the message text, the sender’s Instagram username/ID and the time.

How we use it

Only to answer customers’ questions (price, size, availability, address), to notify the store owner about new comments/messages, and to measure how our own posts perform. We do not use the data for advertising, profiling, or any automated decision with legal effect.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share personal data with third parties. Data is processed on the store owner’s computer and in a private automation environment (GitHub Actions) solely to perform the functions above. Message text may be processed by an AI assistant (Anthropic Claude) only to draft a reply to that customer.

Retention

Comment and message data is kept no longer than 90 days, except aggregated statistics without personal data.

Your rights and deletion

You can ask us to delete any data about you at any time — see Data deletion instructions. We complete requests within 30 days.
